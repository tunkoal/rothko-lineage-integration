# Licensing and source boundaries

New integration scripts and documentation are provided under Apache-2.0; a copy
is in LICENSES/Apache-2.0.txt. Existing files and platform patches retain their
own copyright/license notices. The license text is supplied for files already
identifying those terms and for the new integration material; it is not a
blanket relicensing of device configuration, stock components or third-party code.

Proprietary payloads, kernel/module binaries and the stock firmware archive are
not in these public repositories, including their reachable histories. Their
hashes, source paths, build rules and transformations allow reconstruction from
a user's own matching firmware. Providing a recipe does not grant redistribution
permission for the resulting payloads or establish kernel-source obligations
as satisfied. Do not publish extracted binaries merely because extraction works.

Keep original notices on source and stock-derived integration configuration.
For new contributions state the source and applicable terms; do not replace
notices with a single project-wide claim. Source publication, distribution of
ROM binaries and official LineageOS acceptance are separate decisions.
